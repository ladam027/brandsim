brandsim playground

Paste or upload a small sample of on-brand text. The page writes new sentences in the same voice, then scores each one against your sample using the same method as the brandsim Python tool. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

1. Sample (.txt one per line, or .jsonl with a "text" field)

2. Generate

3. Results

Sentences come from a word-level Markov chain trained only on your sample, and exact copies of sample sentences are thrown out. Score = 0.6 · semantic + 0.25 · ROUGE-L + 0.15 · Jaccard, each taken against the closest sample sentence. Without the semantic score, the weights are renormalised over the two lexical metrics.
